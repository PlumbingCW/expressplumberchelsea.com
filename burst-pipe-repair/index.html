Burst pipe repair in Chelsea, MA is the emergency response to a pipe that has split, cracked or separated and is releasing water into a wall, ceiling, floor or basement. Cold snaps, old corroded pipe and high water pressure are the usual causes.

The first job is stopping the water. Find the main shutoff valve, turn it fully off, open a low faucet to relieve pressure and call our 24/7 line. If water is near electrical panels or outlets, stay clear and switch off power only if you can reach the breaker safely.

First ten minutes after a pipe bursts

Turn off the main shutoff, usually near the meter in the basement. Turn off the water heater, open the lowest faucet to drain the line and move valuables out of the wet area.

Do not use electrical devices while standing in water. Photograph the damage for your insurer, then call us. If you cannot find the main valve, we can talk you through it by phone.

Cold weather and Chelsea's older buildings

Chelsea winters bring hard freezes, and older buildings with exterior wall pipes, unheated basements and drafty stairwells are the first to suffer. A pipe that freezes can split quietly and show the damage only after it thaws.

If a pipe is frozen but not yet burst, open nearby faucets slightly and warm the area gently. Never use an open flame. Call us if you see a bulge, frost on the pipe or no water at a fixture.

After the repair: drying and insurance

Burst pipes soak drywall, insulation and flooring. Standing water and wet materials can grow mold within a couple of days, so professional drying or water damage restoration may be necessary after we fix the pipe.

Homeowner policies often cover sudden pipe bursts but not neglected leaks. Keep photos and our written report, and file your claim promptly. We can document the failed section for the adjuster.

Prevention that fits Chelsea homes

Insulate pipes along exterior walls and in unheated basements, seal drafts near plumbing, and let faucets drip during extreme cold. Know where your main shutoff is and test it once a year so it does not seize.

A pressure check can catch a high-pressure problem before it blows a joint. If you want an inspection, our leak detection service can find stressed areas before they fail.
