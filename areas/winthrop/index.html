Winter on a windy peninsula

Winthrop catches wind off the harbor and the open ocean, and cold snaps hit exterior walls and unheated porches hard. Pipes in older houses near the water freeze and split, and the damage often appears only when they thaw.

Insulate pipes in outside walls, keep cabinet doors open in extreme cold and know where the main shutoff is. If a pipe has split, see burst pipe repair and call us right away.

Spring melt and summer storms

In spring the ground thaws and basements get wet. Summer thunderstorms and tropical remnants can drop heavy rain on a town with limited elevation. A sump pump that has been idle all winter should be tested before the wet season.

Our sump pump service covers testing, repair and backup power, and our leak detection separates plumbing leaks from groundwater.

Winthrop neighborhoods and their plumbing

Winthrop Center and Winthrop Street

Older commercial blocks and apartments near the center. Restaurants rely on grease control and floor drains, and emergency dispatch is available around the clock.

Point Shirley

A low, exposed neighborhood at the southern tip near the harbor. Storm surge and high tides make backflow and sump protection important.

Cottage Park and the Belle Isle side

Older homes on small lots where drain laterals run long and roots find them. Consider a sewer camera inspection.

Winthrop Beach and Shore Drive

Seasonal and year-round homes where exposed outdoor spigots and hose bibs need protection.

Deer Island, the treatment plant and your drains

Deer Island, home to the region's large wastewater treatment plant, is part of Winthrop. Winthrop runs its own public works department for local water and sewer service, and the town's system is separate from Chelsea's.

If the problem is in the street, call the town. If it is inside your house or on your private line, call us, and a camera visit will show which side the blockage is on.
