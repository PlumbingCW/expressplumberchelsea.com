Neighborhoods across East Boston

Each part of the neighborhood has its own building stock and its own typical call.

Maverick Square and Jeffries Point

Older triple-deckers close to the harbor. Aging stacks and shared boilers mean a hot water or heat failure usually affects several apartments. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock.

Eagle Hill and Day Square

Hillside streets where basements and retaining walls manage runoff. Drain backups and slow drains are the common calls.

Orient Heights

Mixed housing near Belle Isle Marsh. Storm water collects in low spots, so sump pump repair matters here.

Bremen Street and Logan edge

Commercial and mixed buildings serving airport traffic need dependable restroom and hot water service.

Triple-deckers and shared systems

The triple-decker is East Boston's signature building. Three apartments share one set of risers, often one boiler and sometimes one water heater, so a single failure becomes three households without heat or hot water.

When we visit, we map which floor feeds which line, then fix the failed part. If the system is near the end of its life, we show you what boiler repair versus replacement looks like before you commit.

Waterfront ground and winter heat

East Boston is low, near the harbor and wrapped around Logan Airport, so storm surge and heavy rain test basements and drains. In winter the same buildings depend on old boilers and exposed pipes along exterior walls.

A burst line near a window or in an unheated hallway needs fast burst pipe repair, and we keep crews ready on freezing nights.

Getting from Chelsea to East Boston

We reach East Boston by the Chelsea Street Bridge or Route 1A. Bridge openings for boat traffic can slow the trip, so dispatch watches for them. If you are headed toward Downtown Boston after the call, we can often fit a second stop through the same tunnels.

Parking on narrow East Boston streets is tight, and we ask you to tell us about any permit or loading zone when you call.

East Boston building types beyond the triple-decker

Newer waterfront condos, converted industrial buildings and older rowhouses each bring different needs. Condo stacks and shared risers require coordination with management, and converted buildings can hide mixed pipe materials from several renovations.

When you call, tell dispatch the building type and the floor. It helps us plan tools and parts. We also keep notes on buildings we have serviced.
