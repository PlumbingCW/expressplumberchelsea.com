Plumbing for homes in Prattville

Prattville is the northwest section of Chelsea, beside the Revere and Everett lines. Washington Avenue runs through it, and the neighborhood is built around two public parks, Voke Park and Washington Park. If your home is near either one, this page is for you.

City planning materials describe Prattville as one of the lower-density parts of Chelsea, with homeownership more common here than downtown. That matters for plumbing because an owner pays for every repair and also makes every call on water heater replacement, drain problems and pipe upgrades.

Landmarks that help place your street

These are easy anchors when you describe your address on the phone.

Washington Avenue

Created in 1641 from a Native American footpath, according to the Discover Chelsea tour of Prattville.

Voke Park

More than 3 acres of open land, named for Edward Voke, who was mayor of Chelsea from 1936 to 1941.

Washington Park

About 1.7 acres bounded by Washington Avenue and Nichols, Franklin and Hancock Streets. It was dedicated on July 4, 1887 and completed in 1897.

Prattville School

Built at 441 Washington Avenue in 1897, closed in 1997 and converted into loft condominiums in 1999.

What the local record does and does not tell us

The Massachusetts Historical Commission report on Chelsea says worker and tenement housing spread up the Mill Creek backslope toward Prattville in the late 19th century, and modest suburban development grew around Washington Square by the early 20th century.

That report dates from 1980 and says nothing about the pipes in any one house. We check the pipes in your building and do not guess from its age. A sewer camera inspection shows the real condition of the line.

If a pipe bursts at home

A house with a basement gives you a clear order of steps.

Close the main

Turn the main water valve clockwise. Practice once now so your hands know it.

Open a tap upstairs

Draining the lines can slow the leak while you wait.

Protect the panel

If water is near electrical equipment, switch off only breakers you can reach dry.

Water and sewer facts for all of Chelsea

Scale is rarely the cause of a problem in Chelsea, because MWRA water is soft at about 1 grain of hardness per gallon. The city also runs a lead service line replacement program, and it reports that roughly 70% of its sewers carry stormwater and sewage together.

These are citywide facts, not Prattville findings. If your water bill jumps, check the low-flow indicator on your meter with every fixture off. A moving indicator means water is escaping, and leak detection can find it.
