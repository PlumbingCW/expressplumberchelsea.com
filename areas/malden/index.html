Water heaters in Malden's older houses

Cellar closets in Malden still hold tanks installed decades ago. Many have rusted through or lost their burners, so no hot water is the complaint that reaches our dispatcher most from this city.

Parts permitting, a swap happens the same day. The replacement page compares a new tank, a hybrid and a tankless unit for a Malden cellar.

Close the cold supply

Switch off the breaker or turn the gas control to off.

Medford and the other neighbors

Malden borders Medford to the northwest, Everett to the south and Melrose to the north. A Medford customer or landlord with a building across the line can mention it and we will schedule both in one trip where possible.

The same crew that serves Malden also serves Chelsea and Everett, so if your problem is one of the common local ones, they have probably seen it three streets away this week.

Drains, roots and old clay

Many Malden laterals are clay, laid in short sections with joints that roots enter. A drain that slows after every storm or clears and returns within a month often means roots or an offset joint.

A hydro jetting visit cuts the roots and a camera shows whether lining or sewer line repair is warranted.

Malden's mix of housing and what it means for plumbing

Malden has older two- and three-family houses, mid-century single-family homes and apartment buildings near the Orange Line. Each type has its own typical failure. Older homes struggle with clay laterals and galvanized supply lines, and apartment buildings with shared water heaters and stacks.

We use the building age and the symptoms you describe to bring the right equipment. A camera, a jetter and a set of common parts usually ride on the truck.

Getting from Chelsea to Malden

The trip from our base to Malden runs north through Everett. Traffic at rush hour can slow the drive, and dispatch will tell you what to expect when you call. We do not promise an arrival time we cannot keep.

If the call is a true emergency, say so at the start and tell dispatch whether water is flowing, whether a gas smell is present and whether anyone is at risk.
