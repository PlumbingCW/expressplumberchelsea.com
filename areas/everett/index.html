What Everett homeowners call about most

Everett has a dense mix of two- and three-family houses, and the calls we see most often start in the kitchen and bathroom drains that stack through those buildings. When a first-floor sink backs up and the unit above has the same problem, the shared line is the likely culprit, and drain cleaning with a camera is the quickest way to prove it.

Leaks are the second most common call. Older supply lines inside walls weep for months before a stain shows on a ceiling, so a leak detection visit often saves a tenant dispute as well as a wall.

If you have a burst pipe or active leak in Everett

Do these four things while the plumber is on the way.

Shut off the main

Turn the main water valve clockwise. In most Everett buildings it sits in the basement near the meter.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets or a panel, switch off those breakers only if you can reach them while dry.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the path of the water and keep children away.
