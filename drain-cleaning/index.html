Drain cleaning in Chelsea, MA means clearing a blocked sink, tub, floor drain or main line so water leaves the building again. A slow drain and a full backup are the same problem at different stages, and the second one can flood a basement in minutes.

If water is already rising, stop running fixtures and call our 24/7 line. While you wait, keep the washer and dishwasher off, move belongings away from floor drains, and keep children and pets out of any standing wastewater.

When a clogged drain becomes an emergency

Most single-fixture clogs can wait until morning, but a few cannot. Water coming up in the tub when the toilet flushes, gurgling from a floor drain, or sewage smell in the basement usually means the shared line is blocked, not one fixture.

Call at night or on a weekend if more than one drain is slow, if wastewater is reaching the floor, or if a ground-floor unit sits below the problem. In Chelsea's older two- and three-story buildings, one stopped stack can push water into every apartment below it.

What blocks drains in Chelsea buildings

Kitchen lines carry the most grease, and the city asks residents to scrape pans into the trash instead of rinsing fat down the sink. That guidance comes from Chelsea's Water and Sewer Department, which also warns against flushing wipes, floss and plastics.

Older multi-family buildings in Mill Hill, Prattville and the Box District often share one stack between floors. Decades of soap scum, hair and roots at joints narrow the pipe until a small clog stops everything.

How we clear a blocked drain

  1. Locate the blockageWe test fixtures one at a time, then look at the cleanout to find out whether the stoppage is in a branch line, the building drain or the lateral.
  2. Snake or cable the lineA motor-driven cable cuts through grease, hair and paper. Bigger cutters handle roots and scale in main lines.
  3. Flush and testWe run water through the line and check every connected fixture for steady flow before we pack up.
  4. Explain what we foundIf the line is cracked, bellied or full of roots, we tell you plainly and give the repair options instead of selling another cleaning.

Kitchen sink clogs and drain line leaks

A kitchen drain clogs faster than any other fixture because grease cools and hardens inside the pipe. We cable past the buildup, then flush with hot water so the line stays open longer than a quick poke would.

Sometimes water appears under a sink or on the ceiling below it after a clog is cleared. That points to a leaking drain joint or cracked fitting, which we repair or replace once the line is open.
