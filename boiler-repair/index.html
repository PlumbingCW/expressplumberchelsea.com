Boiler repair in Chelsea, MA addresses no-heat calls, leaks, strange noises, pressure problems and short cycling in steam and hot water systems. Many older Chelsea buildings are heated by boilers, and they need regular attention in cold months.

If you smell gas or the carbon monoxide alarm sounds, leave the building and call 911 or your gas utility. If there is no heat and no gas smell, call us. If the boiler is leaking, shut the water feed and power to it.

No heat: what to check before you call

Check the thermostat, the boiler switch and the breaker. Look at the pressure gauge. A hot water boiler usually runs between 12 and 20 psi when cold. Low pressure can shut it down.

Do not bleed or refill a system if you are unsure. Dispatch will help you decide whether it is safe to wait for a technician.

Steam and hot water systems in older buildings

Steam boilers rely on proper water level, working vents and pitched pipes. A single failed vent can leave a room cold. Hot water systems rely on circulators, expansion tanks and bled radiators.

Chelsea's older multi-family buildings often have a central boiler. Work needs to be coordinated with the owner and tenants, and the repair record kept for the building.

What to expect from a winter service call

During a cold snap, dispatch handles no-heat calls first and keeps the line moving. We ask about the type of system, the fuel, the age and the symptom so we can bring the right parts. A good description saves a second trip.

If heat cannot be restored the same day, we explain the temporary options for staying warm safely. Do not use an oven or a gas stove to heat a room, because it can release carbon monoxide.

Carbon monoxide and boiler safety

Every home with a fuel-burning boiler should have working carbon monoxide detectors on each level and near sleeping areas. Replace the batteries on a schedule and test the alarms. A detector that sounds means you leave the building and call 911.

We check venting and combustion on each visit. A blocked flue or a cracked heat exchanger is a safety problem, not just a comfort problem, and we tell you plainly when a boiler should be shut down.

Maintenance that helps

A yearly service includes cleaning the burner, checking controls and testing safety devices. In winter, clearing snow from exhaust vents and intake pipes keeps sealed-combustion units running.

If your boiler also feeds an indirect water heater, a boiler failure means no hot water either, and water heater service may overlap with the visit.
