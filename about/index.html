Who we are

Chelsea Express Plumbing is a plumbing company based at 90 Everett Ave in Chelsea, Massachusetts. We take calls around the clock and send a licensed plumber for emergencies, repairs and planned work in Chelsea, Everett, Revere, East Boston, Winthrop and Malden.

We run on the phone. There is no online form to fill out and no waiting for an email reply. You call (617) 752-0573, tell dispatch what is happening and get a straight answer about what we can do and when.

How we work

You get a clear price before work starts. If the job changes once we open a wall or look inside a pipe, we stop, explain what we found and agree on the new scope with you before we continue.

We write down what we find on every call. For landlords, property managers and insurance claims, those notes are often as useful as the repair itself. For the services we cover, see the full services list.

Licensing and permits

Our plumbers are licensed and insured. In Massachusetts, plumbing work generally needs a permit pulled by a licensed plumber, and gas work needs a licensed gas fitter. You can check any plumber's license on the state Division of Occupational Licensure website, and we encourage you to do that with every contractor you hire.

What happens when you call

A person answers and asks four things: the address, what you are seeing, whether water or gas is involved and whether anyone is at risk. Those answers decide how fast we move and what goes on the truck.

If the problem is an active leak, we talk you through the main shutoff while the plumber is on the way. If it is a slow drain or a water heater that has gone cold, we tell you what we can do today and what can wait for a scheduled visit.

The buildings we know

Chelsea is a dense city of two- and three-family houses, brick mixed-use blocks, waterfront warehouses and older apartment buildings. Many have stacked kitchens and bathrooms, shared basements and boilers or water heaters serving more than one household.

That shapes how we work. We ask about the floor and the other units, shut off only what we must and leave notes the owner can read. Neighborhoods such as Bellingham Square, the Box District, Mill Hill and the Waterfront District each have their own mix of buildings, and our crew has worked in all of them.

What we do not do

We do not run forms or chat bots. We do not quote a price we cannot stand behind, and we do not push a replacement when a repair will do. We also do not claim credentials, awards or review counts we cannot show you.

If a job is outside plumbing, such as electrical work or drywall repair, we say so and suggest you hire the right trade. If a problem needs a specialist, such as a gas utility or the city water department, we point you there.

Questions people ask before they call

Do you come at night? Yes, the line is open 24/7. Do you serve my town? If it is Chelsea, Everett, Revere, East Boston, Winthrop or Malden, yes, and we will talk through anything farther away. Can I book online? No, we work by phone so you reach a person right away.

Do you handle commercial buildings? Yes, from restaurants to multi-unit properties, and our commercial plumbing page explains how. If you are comparing us with others, ask each company for its Massachusetts license type and check it with the state before you hire anyone.

How we keep prices clear

A price should not be a surprise. For most calls, the plumber looks at the problem, tells you what is wrong and gives you a number before opening a toolbox. If the work grows, we stop and talk before going on.

We write down what was done, what parts were used and what we found. Landlords and property managers use those notes for tenants and records, and homeowners use them for insurers. Our service pages list what moves the price for each kind of job so you know what to expect before you call.

If you want a second opinion on a quote from someone else, call and describe it. We will tell you whether the scope looks reasonable for the problem, even if you end up hiring someone else for the work.
