Pipe Lining Installation in Chelsea, MA: Homeowner Guide

    Talk to a plumber
    (617) 752-0573
    Call Now

    Written by the Chelsea Express Plumbing team. Published 2026-11-16. Updated 2026-11-16.

    Pipe lining installation is the day when the new liner goes into the old pipe. Before that day, there is planning: a camera inspection, a cleaning, a permit and a decision about how to handle laterals and access points.

    This guide follows the installation from the first call to the final camera pass, and helps you decide whether lining is right for your building.

    Before installation day

    We run a camera through the line to confirm the length, diameter and condition. Roots and scale are cut away first, because a liner needs a clean wall. We then plan access points, and any bends that might be an issue.

    You choose whether to line one section, such as a basement run, or the whole lateral to the street. The camera shows where the damage is, which helps with that choice.

    Installation day

    1. Set up accessWe open a cleanout or remove a fixture to reach the line.
    2. Prepare the linerA resin-coated tube is cut to length and wet out on site.
    3. Insert and inflateThe liner is pulled or inverted through the pipe and pressed against the wall.
    4. CureSteam, hot water or light cures the resin into a hard pipe.
    5. Reopen laterals and inspectCutters reopen side connections and a camera confirms the result.

    What can affect scheduling

    Factors that influence timing
    FactorEffect
    Roots or heavy scaleExtra cleaning time
    Multiple bends or reductionsPlanning for liner fit
    Active water useBuilding must limit use during cure
    WeatherAffects outdoor access and curing
    Permit and inspectionLead time with the city

    Living in the building during the work

    You will need to avoid using drains for several hours while the liner cures. We tell you in advance and give you a time window. In multi-unit buildings, we coordinate with each household.

    Keep pets and children away from the work area. The resin has a smell during curing, and we ventilate the area.

    Comparing lining with replacement

    Lining is faster and creates less mess. Replacement gives you a new full-size pipe, and it is the only choice for collapsed or separated pipe. The right answer depends on the camera.

    We explain both in plain language and give you a written scope for each. Read more on sewer line repair.

    Chelsea local note

    Chelsea's sewer system is about 70 percent combined, and the city continues its work on separating storm and sanitary lines. A sound lateral keeps groundwater out and reduces your contribution to storm flows.

    Massachusetts requires a plumbing permit for sewer work, issued to a licensed plumber. Source types: city sewer information and state plumbing licensing guidance.

    Preparing the building for lining day

    Tell everyone in the building about the time window. Ask them to avoid running water, flushing toilets or using the dishwasher while the liner cures. A sign on the door of each unit helps. Make sure the cleanouts are reachable and the area around them is clear.

    Park the vehicles that might block access, and keep pets away from the work area.

    What can go wrong and how we handle it

    A liner can fail to seat if a pipe is out of round or if a sudden cold snap slows the cure. We watch the temperature and the cure time and stop if conditions are wrong. If a liner does not cure properly, it is cut out and redone.

    We record the camera pass after the job and show you the footage. If there is a defect, we point it out and explain the next step.

    Aftercare for a lined pipe

    Treat the line as you would any sewer. Avoid grease, wipes and heavy chemicals, and have it checked with a camera if you see a slow drain. A lined pipe is smooth, but it can still block with paper and debris.

    Keep the final recording and the permit paperwork. They help when you sell the property or deal with an insurer.

    Documentation and records for the building file

    Keep the permit, the pre- and post-lining camera recordings, the product data and the invoice together. A buyer's inspector or a lender may ask for them, and an insurer may want them after a claim. Digital copies stored in two places are best.

    We provide the recordings and a short written summary of the work. If the line is shared with a neighbor, a copy also goes to the other owner, so both sides know what was done and where the liner begins and ends.

    What lining does not change

    Lining does not move a pipe, raise a sagging section or fix a poorly designed layout. If the original run had a bad slope, the liner follows it. Lining also does not add a cleanout where none exists, though we can often add one during the same visit.

    Think of it as a new interior surface for the pipe you already have. When the layout itself is the problem, replacement is the honest answer, and we explain why.
