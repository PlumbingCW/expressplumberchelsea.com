Toilet repair in Chelsea, MA covers the common failures: a flapper that does not seal, a fill valve that never shuts off, a loose base with a failed wax ring, a cracked tank and a clog deeper than a plunger can reach.

If a toilet is overflowing, shut the small valve on the wall behind it by turning it clockwise. Then call our 24/7 line. If several fixtures back up at once, the problem is the main line, not the toilet.

An overflowing toilet: what to do right now

Lift the tank lid and push the flapper closed, or turn the supply valve behind the toilet until the water stops. Lay towels around the base and keep children and pets away from the water.

Do not keep flushing. If other toilets or the basement floor drain are also gurgling, stop using water in the building and call us, because the blockage is likely downstream in the drain line.

Older Chelsea bathrooms and old flanges

Many Chelsea buildings have older bathroom floors where the toilet flange has rusted, cracked or been set at the wrong height. A new toilet on a broken flange will keep leaking no matter how many wax rings you try.

We check the flange and floor before setting a toilet. If the subfloor is soft from a slow leak, we tell you before we cover it up.

A typical toilet repair visit

  1. DiagnoseWe test the flush, check the fill and look for leaks at the base and supply.
  2. Repair the partFlapper, fill valve, handle, supply line or wax ring, whichever is the real fault.
  3. Clear a blockageWe use a closet auger or camera if the clog is beyond the trap.
  4. Test and clean upWe flush repeatedly, check for leaks and leave the bathroom clean.

When to replace instead of repair

Replace a toilet that is cracked, has a damaged glaze, flushes poorly despite repairs or wastes a lot of water per flush. Modern high-efficiency toilets use far less water and clear waste better than older models.

Chelsea households on a metered supply feel that difference on every bill. If you are choosing a new unit, we can help you pick a height and bowl shape that fits your bathroom, including smart toilets where the electrical and water supply allow.
