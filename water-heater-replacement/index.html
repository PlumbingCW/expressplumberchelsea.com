Water heater replacement in Chelsea, MA means removing a failed or end-of-life heater and installing a new one that fits the space, fuel and household. A tank that leaks at the base, a heater that is well past its expected service life or one that needs repeated repairs is a candidate.

If the tank is flooding the floor, shut the cold water supply valve above it and turn off power or gas. Then call our 24/7 line, because the faster a replacement is scheduled, the shorter the time without hot water.

Emergency tank failure: what to do and how fast we can help

Close the cold water supply to the heater, switch off the breaker or gas control, and drain a bit of the tank only if you can do so safely. Move stored items away from the base and keep floor drains clear.

We offer same-day appointments when available and will tell you at the first call whether a replacement is possible today. We do not promise an arrival window we cannot meet.

Fitting a heater into a Chelsea building

In older three-deckers and brick buildings, the heater often sits in a tight basement closet or a utility room shared with a boiler. Clearance, flue routing and access to shutoff valves all need to be checked before the new unit goes in.

Condos and multi-unit buildings add another layer. Shared stacks, building rules and where the unit is allowed to vent can limit the choices, so we check with the owner or association where it applies.

Permits and licensing for a replacement

In Massachusetts, replacing or relocating a water heater, valves or piping requires a permit from the local plumbing inspector, and only a licensed master or journeyman plumber can obtain it. Gas connections must follow the state plumbing and gas code.

Ask to see the permit when work is done, because the permit is your assurance the job will be inspected. You can also verify any plumber's license through the state Division of Occupational Licensure.

Repair or replace: a quick way to decide

If the tank is leaking, replace it. If the heater is older and has needed more than one repair, the next failure is often close and replacement usually costs less than repeated service.

A newer heater with one failed part is better repaired, and water heater repair is the right call. We will not push a replacement we cannot justify.
