Commercial plumbing in Chelsea, MA covers the plumbing in restaurants, retail space, offices, warehouses and apartment buildings. The work includes drain and sewer clearing, leak repair, water heater service, grease interceptor maintenance and backflow prevention testing.

Chelsea has a dense mix of food service, produce and distribution businesses and multi-family buildings. Downtime costs money, so we focus on response, clear communication and fixes that last.

When a business plumbing problem is an emergency

A backed-up kitchen drain, a failed restroom or a leak above stock or equipment cannot wait for next week. Shut the water to the affected area if you can, move product and call our line.

We ask for the building address, the affected area and whether the problem affects tenants or neighbors, so the right tools arrive on the first trip.

Kitchen drains often need hydro jetting instead of snaking, because grease builds up along the pipe wall.

Grease traps and the city sewer

Chelsea's sewer system is mostly combined, so grease that gets into the line contributes to blockages that affect neighbors, and the city and MWRA expect food service businesses to maintain their grease controls.

Pumping and cleaning a grease trap on a regular schedule is cheaper than an emergency clog on a Saturday night. We help you match service frequency to your kitchen volume.

Backflow prevention and testing

A backflow preventer stops contaminated water from flowing back into the supply. Many commercial and multi-unit buildings must have the device tested on a regular schedule, and the results reported to the local water authority.

Ask the Chelsea Water and Sewer Department what is required for your property. We test and repair devices and can replace a failed unit.

Property managers and landlords with several buildings

If you manage more than one property, tell dispatch which buildings you own and who the contact is at each. We keep notes on the shutoffs, the water heaters and the history of each building, so the second call is faster than the first.

We send written findings after each visit, which helps with tenant disputes and insurance questions. Plan maintenance visits for slower months, and keep emergency calls for real emergencies.

Restaurants and food service

A kitchen depends on hot water, floor drains, hand sinks and a grease system that works every shift. Health inspections look at all four. A slow floor drain or a lukewarm hand sink can cost a failed inspection and a lost night of service.

We recommend a regular walkthrough of the kitchen plumbing, with notes on what is aging, so repairs happen on your schedule.
