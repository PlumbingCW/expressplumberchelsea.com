Sewer line cleaning in Chelsea, MA clears the main building drain, the pipe that carries everything from your plumbing to the connection under the street. When that line stops, the first sign is usually a basement floor drain bubbling or a first-floor toilet that will not clear.

Water in the basement from the sewer is an emergency. Stop using plumbing, stay out of the standing water, and call our 24/7 line so a plumber can open the line before more wastewater arrives.

Main line or city sewer: who fixes what

Chelsea reports that about 70 percent of the city sits on combined sewers, where stormwater and wastewater share one pipe. Heavy rain can slow a combined main, so a backup during a storm is not always a blockage on your side.

A useful test is to ask neighbors on your street whether they are backing up too. If they are, call the Water and Sewer Department about the street main. If only your building is affected, the blockage is almost certainly in your own lateral and we can clear it.

Main line backup: signs you should not wait on

One slow sink is a branch problem. Several fixtures that slow together, a toilet that bubbles when the washer drains, or a floor drain that fills after a shower point to the main.

We confirm with a cable at the cleanout and a camera if the line has a history. The camera also finds a low spot, called a belly, where waste settles and builds a blockage again and again.

How we open and clean a main line

  1. Protect the spaceWe lay down covers, check the cleanout and set the equipment so nothing splashes into the living space.
  2. Cut through the blockageA large cable with a cutter head breaks up the stoppage and pulls it back out of the line.
  3. Clean the pipe wallWe follow with a jetter or scraper head where grease or scale has narrowed the pipe.
  4. Verify by flow and cameraWater is run from the highest fixture while we watch the cleanout, and a camera runs the line if it keeps failing.

Storm drains and catch basins

Private storm drains on lots and driveways clog with leaves and grit. If a yard drain overflows during rain while the house sewer is fine, the drain needs cleaning, not the main.

The city cleans catch basins in the street, so report a blocked street grate to the Department of Public Works. We handle the drains on your side of the property line.
