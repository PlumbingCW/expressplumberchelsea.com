Water Service Line Repair and Replacement in Chelsea, MA

A water service line is the buried pipe that brings water from the city main in the street to your building. Water service line repair in Chelsea, MA covers leaks, breaks, low pressure from a failing line and the replacement of old lead or galvanized lines.

The line is split into two parts. The utility owns the section from the main to the curb, and the property owner is responsible for the private side from the curb to the meter. That boundary shapes who calls whom.

Signs your service line is failing

A sudden drop in pressure, rust or discolored water, a wet or sunken patch in the yard, a meter that spins with all taps off and a rising water bill each point to a service line problem.

These symptoms overlap with interior leaks, so we start by confirming where the loss is. Our leak detection service separates a service line leak from a plumbing leak inside.

Lead service lines in Chelsea

Chelsea has a lead service line replacement program, and the city asks residents to find out what material their service line is made of. Older lines in some buildings are lead or galvanized steel.

A plumber can identify the line material where it enters the building and tell you whether it needs attention. If you have a lead line, coordinate with the city's Water and Sewer Department so the public and private sides are handled together where possible.

Digging in a dense city

Chelsea's tight lots, narrow streets and sidewalk-to-building setbacks make excavation harder than in suburban yards. We call Dig Safe before we dig, plan around other utilities and may need street or sidewalk permits through the city.

Trenchless methods can reduce disruption where pavement, trees or retaining walls are in the way, though they are not suitable in every soil or layout.
