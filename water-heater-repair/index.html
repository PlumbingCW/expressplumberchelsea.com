Water heater repair in Chelsea, MA covers the part failures that leave a home without hot water or leaking onto a floor. Typical causes are a failed thermostat or heating element, a pilot or ignition fault, a bad gas valve or a tank that has begun to leak.

If you see water around the tank, turn off the cold supply valve above it and switch the heater off at the breaker or gas control. If you smell gas, leave the building, call your gas utility or 911 and only then call us.

No hot water or a leak: what to do first

A tank that is dripping from the bottom is usually past repair, but one leaking from a fitting or the relief valve can be fixed. Turn off the heater and the cold water supply to it, then put a bucket under any drip.

Do not touch wet wiring. If the heater sits in a basement with standing water around it, wait for a plumber before you step near it, and call our line any hour to get someone on the way.

Why water heaters struggle in Chelsea basements

Many Chelsea buildings keep the heater in a small basement utility area with limited clearance and old venting. Cramped space makes sediment flushing and valve access harder, and old flues sometimes need to be updated before a repair is safe.

The water itself is easy on tanks. MWRA supplies soft water at about 1 grain per gallon of hardness, so Chelsea tanks suffer less scale than hard-water regions, though sediment and corrosion still show up with age.

Gas, electric and hybrid units

Gas heaters need a clean burner, a working thermocouple and a flue that draws properly. A pilot that keeps going out usually points to a weak thermocouple, a dirty orifice or a draft problem in the vent.

Electric units lose hot water when an upper or lower heating element fails. Hybrid heat pump models add a compressor and fans, so their faults can also be airflow or condensate problems, and they need to be sized into a space with enough air.

Tankless heater faults and flushing

A tankless unit that shows an error code or runs lukewarm often needs descaling, a cleaned filter or a venting check. Flushing the heat exchanger on a schedule keeps flow and efficiency steady, and tankless water heater service covers the details.

Callers often tell us a previous company said they only replace tankless units. We service them and tell you plainly when a unit is past saving.
